PART 01

The Facebook Algorithm Reality (Why 90% of Posts Get Buried)

As the founder of Florida Real Estate and Open Houses, I review hundreds of posts every week. The single biggest reason great properties get zero comments is because the agent drops a naked MLS link in the caption. Here is how Facebook's feed algorithm actually works:

1

Rule 1: Never Put Outbound Links in the Caption

Facebook is an advertising business that keeps users inside their app. When you paste an external MLS link or Zillow URL in the main caption, Facebook classifies it as an outbound exit link and suppresses the reach by up to 90%. Instead, upload 4 native photos and place your virtual tour or MLS search portal in the first comment.

2

Rule 2: State the Price and City Transparently

Hiding the price with "DM for price" triggers immediate skepticism. 90% of qualified local buyers scroll past because they assume it is overpriced. Clear price transparency establishes you as an honest, confident professional.

3

Rule 3: Use a Single-Word Comment Trigger

Instead of "Hope to see you there!", end your post with: "Drop a comment with the word DETAILS for room dimensions, average electric bills, and private tour availability." When 10 to 20 users leave a single-word comment, Facebook interprets the post as viral community content and pushes it into thousands of member feeds!

PART 02

3 High-Converting Post Frameworks That Actually Sound Human

Notice the complete absence of cheesy real estate clichés. These frameworks sound like an authentic local expert sharing real lifestyle insights with a neighbor.

FRAMEWORK 01

The "Candid Neighborhood Walkthrough" Hook

Best for Open Houses

When to post: Thursday or Friday morning. Instead of shouting "Come see my open house!", it addresses a real buyer frustration (like finding a single-story pool home with no HOA) and shares honest takeaways from walking through the property.

If you have been looking for a single-story pool home in [City / Neighborhood] that doesn't have an HOA breathing down your neck, you already know how rare they are right now.

We are holding an open house this Saturday from [11:00 AM to 2:00 PM] at [Address].

A couple honest takeaways from walking this property:
- The backyard is completely private. You sit on the screened lanai looking at mature palms and green space, not straight into your neighbor's living room.
- The kitchen was redone properly: real soft-close wood cabinets, quartz counters, and a layout where you can cook while friends hang out around the island.
- The oversized garage has room for a boat, golf cart, or workshop with no HOA telling you no.

Offered at $[Price].

If you live in the neighborhood, stop by to say hello (cold drinks and snacks on the counter). If you want the survey, room dimensions, or seller disclosures before the weekend, drop a comment with "INFO" and I'll message it right to you.

FRAMEWORK 02

The "Market Reality Check" Price Drop Hook

Best for Price Reductions

When to post: Immediately following a price reduction. By connecting the price drop to monthly mortgage savings and move-in ready peace of mind, buyers stop and recalculate their monthly numbers.

The sellers listened to the market. We just adjusted the price by $[Amount Dropped] on this [Bed/Bath count] home in [Neighborhood, City], bringing it to $[New Price].

At this new number, this is officially one of the lowest price-per-square-foot homes in [Area] that has a private pool and brand-new roof.

Why this matters for your monthly budget:
- At the old price, buyers were hesitating on the rate math. At $[New Price], the monthly payment drops by roughly $[Estimated Savings] / month.
- It is genuinely move-in ready: new 2026 roof, updated HVAC, and zero deferred maintenance for you to worry about.
- Located just [Number] minutes from [Beach / Downtown / A-rated schools].

We are scheduling private walkthroughs all week before the weekend open house.

If you want the seller disclosures, average monthly utility costs, or survey sent over, leave a comment with "WALKTHROUGH" or message me directly.

Listed by: [Your Name] | [Your Brokerage] | [License #]
FRAMEWORK 03

The "Candid Numbers Breakdown" for Condotels & STRs

Best for Cash Flow Investors

When to post: Anytime you have a condo, condotel, or vacation property. Investors don't want fluff or adjectives; they want rental regulations, management flexibility, and trailing gross revenue numbers.

Most buyers looking at coastal condos in [City / Area] run into the exact same two headaches: strict 30-day rental caps or massive hidden special assessments.

This unit at [Building / Condo Name] is one of the rare exceptions.

Here is the straightforward breakdown:
- Daily short-term rentals are 100% allowed (self-manage on Airbnb/VRBO or use on-site front desk management).
- Historical gross revenue: This floorplan averaged $[Historical Gross Amount] gross over the last 12 months.
- The view: You are on the [Floor #] floor with direct ocean views from the living room and balcony.
- Completely furnished turnkey: heated oceanfront pool, fitness room, and direct beach access.

Offered at $[Price].

If you want the 12-month trailing revenue statement, monthly HOA line-item breakdown, and pro forma numbers, comment "PROFORMA" below and I'll send the PDF directly to you.

Tested AI Prompts That Sound 100% Human

Paste these prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini alongside your raw MLS property notes to generate authentic, high-converting social copy in under 30 seconds.

AI PROMPT 01

High-CTR Facebook Marketplace Listing Generator

Marketplace Hook

Engineered with our Human Voice Protocol to ban generic real estate clichés and focus on tangible everyday lifestyle specifics.

You are an elite real estate copywriter. Take the following property specs and write a high-converting Facebook Marketplace listing description:

[PASTE PROPERTY ADDRESS, PRICE, BEDS/BATHS, SQFT, AND TOP 3 FEATURES]

RULES FOR OUTPUT:
1. Zero robotic AI clichés: NEVER use "nestled", "boasts", "oasis", "breathtaking", "rare gem", or "delve".
2. Write in a warm, direct, first-person tone. Sounds like a knowledgeable local agent texting a friend.
3. Highlight 3 practical lifestyle details (e.g. natural morning light, backyard privacy, room for a boat).
4. End with a low-friction trigger word: "Send a message with the word DETAILS for room dimensions and private tour availability."
5. Include standard license compliance: Listed by [Name], [Brokerage].
