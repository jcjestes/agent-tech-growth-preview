GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

You can ask
the simple questions.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We start with what you already understand and connect new tools to familiar tasks. You don’t need a technical background.

Do I have to join eXp to watch your videos?

No. The educational videos are intended to be useful on their own. Ongoing organization guidance is a separate conversation, not a condition for watching.

What’s included in the free guidance?

Guidance for agents in our organization can cover AI, marketing ideas, CRM workflows, business systems, and how to take advantage of the training stack. We’ll clarify availability and scope together. Paid software and custom builds are not automatically included.

What if I am already tech-savvy?

Great. This is also for agents who already like technology and want like-minded agents to bounce ideas off of. We can talk about tools, automations, content systems, AI workflows, and what you are trying to build.
