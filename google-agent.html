HOW IT WORKS

The 3 Pillars of Agency-Grade Inbound Real Estate

Before I got into real estate, I built and ran an international digital marketing agency employing hundreds of specialists. Here is the exact organic SEO and video architecture that makes real estate easy:

01
PILLAR 01: HYPERLOCAL SEARCH

Dominating the Google Local 3-Pack in Your Market

When high-net-worth buyers and relocating sellers search for real estate help, they don't look at billboards. They open Google Maps on their phone and search: "Best Realtor in [City] Florida" or "Top real estate agent in [Neighborhood]".

The Agency Play: Most agents register a Google Business Profile once and never touch it. We show partner agents how to optimize geo-tagged property photos, build verified local citations, and deploy automated 5-star review request sequences so you rank in the top 3 spots on Google Maps where 70% of phone calls happen.
02
PILLAR 02: VIDEO SEARCH ENGINE

The 3 YouTube Videos That Relocation Buyers Watch

YouTube is the second-largest search engine in the world and owned by Google. When an out-of-state family in New York, Ohio, or California decides to move to Florida, they spend months watching YouTube videos before they ever contact an agent.

The 3 Essential Video Frameworks We Give You:
• The "Living In [City]" Reality Check: The honest pros and cons of moving to your market (traffic, weather, insurance, beaches).
• The Neighborhood Driving Tour: Drive through 3 distinct communities with a dashboard camera, explaining HOA fees, amenities, and price points.
• The Cost of Living Breakdown: Real utility bills, property taxes, and insurance estimates that prove you are the definitive local authority.
03
PILLAR 03: CONVERSION AUTOMATION

Closing Inbound Relocation Calls with BoldTrail & AI

When an out-of-state buyer reaches out from YouTube or Google, you don't need to chase them. They already feel like they know you. But speed-to-lead and structured follow-up are what turn conversations into signed buyer agreements.

The Tech Stack: At eXp Realty, every agent receives BoldTrail CRM & Custom Consumer MLS Website included ($0 added software fee). We plug in our tested smart campaigns and the 120 AI Mega Prompt Vault to send personalized neighborhood guides and saved MLS searches in under 90 seconds.
