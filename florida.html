THE COASTAL REALITY CHECK

Why Traditional Florida Prospecting Is Breaking Down

If you have been selling real estate in coastal Florida for more than six months, you already know the ground has shifted:

1. Out-of-State Buyers Do Not Answer Cold Calls:

The highest-net-worth buyers moving from New York, Ohio, California, and New Jersey are researching neighborhoods, tax rates, and coastal living on YouTube and Google months before they ever book a flight. If you do not have video authority online, you are invisible to them.

2. Portal Leads Are a Low-Margin Trap:

Paying $150 to $200 for a recycled Zillow lead where five other agents are calling the exact same consumer within 30 seconds is a race to the bottom. Inbound video brings buyers who call YOU pre-sold.

3. Coastal Complexities Require Specialist Authority:

Between SB 4-D condo milestone inspections, flood zones, roof age restrictions, and condotel lending rules, buyers want a seasoned local advisor who understands the Florida numbers.
