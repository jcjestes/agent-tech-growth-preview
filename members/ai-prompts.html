Listing Marketing

Compelling Listing Description Generator

Turns bullet points of home features into an emotional, buyer-focused property story.

Act as an expert real estate copywriter. Write a compelling, immersive listing description for a home with the following details: - Property Type: [Single Family Home / Condo / Townhouse] - Location: [Neighborhood, City, State] - Key Specs: [Beds / Baths / Sq Ft / Lot Size] - Standout Features: [e.g. Chef's kitchen, custom pool, primary suite with spa bath, 3-car garage] - Vibe / Lifestyle: [e.g. Coastal entertaining, quiet cul-de-sac, luxury living] Structure the description with: 1. A punchy headline hook that highlights the primary benefit. 2. An engaging 2-paragraph narrative focusing on the lifestyle and flow of the home. 3. A clean bulleted list of high-value upgrades. 4. A clear call to action to schedule a private tour. Avoid cliches like "must see" or "won't last long." Keep the tone refined and inviting.
Social Media

7-Day Local Authority Content Calendar

Generates a complete week of hyper-local social media posts designed to start conversations.

Act as a top social media strategist for real estate agents. Create a 7-day organic social media content calendar for [City / County, State]. The goal is to generate local buyer and seller conversations without cold pitching. Include for each day: - Content Pillar (e.g. Local Spotlight, Market Stat, Behind the Scenes, Client Win, Buyer/Seller Education) - The Hook / Headline - Brief Post Caption Outline - Recommended Visual Format (Square Photo, Carousel, or 30-Second Reel) - Specific Call to Action (e.g. "Comment below", "DM me for the guide") Keep the tone approachable, expert, and focused on hyper-local community life.
Client Follow-Up

Stale Lead Revival Text & Email Sequence

Re-engages buyer and seller leads who went dark 3 to 12 months ago without being pushy.

Act as a top real estate coach. Write a 3-part follow-up sequence (2 text messages and 1 short email) to re-engage prospective home buyers who went cold several months ago. Requirements: - Low pressure and conversational. - No guilt-tripping or asking "are you still looking?" - Offer something of specific, timely value (e.g. a new off-market list, an updated neighborhood price report, or a recent mortgage rate drop). - Keep each text under 35 words. - Keep the email under 90 words with a compelling, non-salesy subject line.
Seller Conversion

CMA Executive Summary for Sellers

Translates complex MLS sales comparables into a clean, easy-to-read pricing strategy letter.

Act as a high-producing listing agent. Write an executive summary letter to accompany a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) for a prospective home seller. Seller Name: [Seller Name] Property Address: [Property Address] Key Recent Comps Summary: [e.g. 3 homes sold in the last 60 days between $575k and $620k with avg 22 days on market] Recommended Pricing Strategy: [e.g. List at $595,000 to drive multiple showing bids in week one] Write a professional, reassuring 3-paragraph letter explaining: 1. How local buyer demand is responding to properties in their specific neighborhood right now. 2. The logic behind the recommended pricing range (avoiding overpricing traps). 3. The marketing plan we will execute in week one to maximize exposure.
Video Marketing

60-Second Short Form Reel Script

Creates a high-retention video script for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or TikTok.

Write a 60-second talking-head video script for a real estate agent targeting [Buyers / Sellers] in [Market Name]. Topic: [e.g. Why waiting for interest rates to drop could cost you $40,000 in home appreciation] Format the script with: - [0:00 - 0:05] Strong visual and verbal HOOK that stops the scroll. - [0:05 - 0:20] THE PROBLEM: The common misconception people have right now. - [0:20 - 0:45] THE SOLUTION / DATA: Concrete numbers or local insight showing what really happens. - [0:45 - 0:60] CALL TO ACTION: A natural invitation to message or comment for the full breakdown. Include camera direction notes and on-screen text suggestions.
Objection Handling

Empathetic Objection Handler

Provides thoughtful, consultative responses to the most common buyer and seller hesitations.

Act as an empathetic, consultative real estate advisor. Give me 3 different conversation responses to handle the following common client objection: Objection: "[e.g. We love the house, but we want to wait until mortgage rates come down further.]" Provide: 1. The Empathetic Acknowledgement: Validates their concern without being defensive. 2. The Perspective Shift: Shares market data or historical context that re-frames the situation. 3. The Low-Pressure Question: An open-ended question that helps them think through the real cost of waiting versus moving forward. Avoid canned, high-pressure salesperson cliches. Keep it human, educational, and consultative.
