Listing Acquisition

Expired listing strategy & letters.

Sellers of expired listings are motivated, but they are frustrated and skeptical. Use this consultative diagnosis approach and direct mail letter to win their trust without sounding like an aggressive telemarketer.

The 3 Ps Diagnosis (Why It Didn't Sell)

When a home fails to sell in our market, it is never a mystery. It always comes down to one of the 3 Ps:

  • 1. Price: Was the home positioned ahead of the market, forcing motivated buyers to choose better-priced competitors?
  • 2. Presentation: Were the photos low quality, dark, or cluttered? Did the home lack professional staging or floor plans?
  • 3. Promotion: Did the previous agent merely put the property on the MLS and pray, or did they run targeted digital video, social syndication, and agent-to-agent outreach?

The Consultative Expired Letter Template

Send this typewritten letter in a handwritten envelope directly to the homeowner:

Dear [Homeowner Name], I am writing to you because I noticed your home at [Property Address] recently came off the market. I know you are likely receiving a flood of aggressive phone calls from real estate agents asking for your listing. That is not why I am reaching out. When a beautiful home in [Neighborhood] does not sell during its listing period, it is almost never because buyers don't want to live there. In our experience, it comes down to three factors: pricing strategy, architectural presentation, or proactive marketing reach. I prepared a confidential "3-P Analysis" for your specific property comparing how your home was presented against the homes that successfully closed in [Neighborhood] over the last 90 days. If you are still planning on moving and would like an honest, second opinion on what it will take to get your home sold, I would be happy to share this analysis with you over a 15-minute phone call. No pressure, and no obligations. Feel free to call or text me directly at [Phone Number], or email me at [Email Address]. Sincerely, [Your Name] Broker Associate | eXp Realty RealWolfPack.com

Conversational Phone / Door Script

When speaking directly with the homeowner:

"Hi [Name], my name is [Your Name] with eXp Realty. I noticed your home on [Street] came off the market today and wanted to see: are you folks still planning on selling and moving, or are you taking a break from the market? (If still planning on selling): 'I completely understand. If I could show you a completely different marketing plan that creates fresh buyer competition without dropping your price through the floor, would you be open to an honest 15-minute conversation?'"
