Sequence 1: Buyer Welcome & Strategy Drip (3 Emails)

Email 1 · Immediately After Inquiry
Subject: Quick question about your home search in [City]
Hi [First Name], Thanks for reaching out about homes in [City / Market]! Home shopping online is a great place to start, but Zillow and Realtor.com often display properties that are already under contract or have pending offers. To make sure I only send you properties that actually fit your criteria: 1. What is your ideal timeframe for moving? 2. Are you focused on a specific school district, neighborhood, or lifestyle (e.g. pool, acreage, golf, water)? Hit reply and let me know. I will set up a clean, direct MLS portal for you so you see new listings the second they hit the market, before the public portals update. Best regards, [Your Name] [Phone Number] [Your Brokerage]
Email 2 · Day 3 Post-Inquiry
Subject: The biggest mistake buyers make before touring homes
Hi [First Name], Over the past few years, the number one mistake I see excited buyers make is touring homes before having a fully verified pre-approval in hand. Here is why that matters: In our local market, the most desirable homes often receive interest within the first few days of listing. If you find your dream home on a Saturday afternoon, you cannot submit an enforceable offer without a lender pre-approval letter attached. A strong pre-approval letter does two things: 1. It shows the seller you have the cash and credit to close, giving you serious negotiating leverage. 2. It gives you 100% clarity on your monthly payment so there are no surprises at the closing table. If you don't have a trusted local lender yet, let me know. I can introduce you to two reputable mortgage specialists who offer competitive rates and close on time. Best regards, [Your Name] [Phone Number]
Email 3 · Day 7 Post-Inquiry
Subject: Off-market homes in [Market Name]
Hi [First Name], Wanted to share a quick update on inventory in [Market Name]. A lot of the best deals never hit the major public websites right away. Through our local network and off-market outreach, we frequently learn about homes 1 to 2 weeks before they go active on the MLS. If you are open to seeing off-market or upcoming listings that match your criteria, reply to this email with: - Target price range - Minimum bedrooms / bathrooms - Preferred towns or neighborhoods I will check our private inventory list and send over any matches. Best regards, [Your Name] [Phone Number]

Sequence 2: Seller Consultation & Pricing Drip (2 Emails)

Email 1 · Confirmation of Appointment
Subject: Confirming our walkthrough on [Day] + 3 things to prepare
Hi [Seller Name], Looking forward to meeting with you on [Day] at [Time] at [Property Address]! Our walkthrough will take about 30 minutes. My goal is simple: learn about your timeline, tour the home, and walk you through a customized Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) so you understand exactly what local buyers are paying right now. To make the most of our time, having these items handy is helpful (but not required): 1. A quick list of any major upgrades or replacements you have done (roof, AC, appliances, flooring). 2. Approximate monthly utility costs. 3. Your ideal timeline for closing and moving. See you on [Day]! Best regards, [Your Name] [Phone Number]
Email 2 · Day After Consultation
Subject: Next steps & marketing plan for [Property Address]
Hi [Seller Name], Thank you for opening your home to me yesterday. I loved touring [Property Address] and discussing your plans. As we discussed, our marketing strategy is designed to create maximum buyer urgency in week one: - Professional architectural photography & drone video - Targeted social media campaigns reaching local move-up buyers - High-intent email broadcast to top local buyer agents - Dedicated property landing page and syndication to all major portals I have attached our updated pricing strategy summary and pre-listing preparation checklist. Take a look, and let me know if you have any questions. Whenever you are ready to move forward, we can finalize the paperwork and schedule photography! Best regards, [Your Name] [Phone Number]
