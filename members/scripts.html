Consultative Dialogue

Real estate scripts & objection handlers.

No high-pressure sales tricks. These consultative scripts help you handle fee objections, price reductions, and hesitant buyers with confidence and professionalism.

Fee Objection

"Will you reduce your commission?"

When a seller asks for a discount at the listing table. Focus on negotiation ability.

"I appreciate you asking that. Let me ask you: if an agent folds and discounts their own fee within 30 seconds of meeting you, how aggressively do you think they are going to defend your equity when a tough buyer agent tries to negotiate $30,000 off your asking price? My full fee covers professional photography, targeted social syndication, and seasoned contract negotiation. My goal isn't to be the cheapest agent in town. My goal is to put the highest net check in your pocket at closing."
Market Timing

"We want to wait until mortgage rates come down."

When buyers are sitting on the fence waiting for lower rates.

"I completely understand why that feels like the safer move. But consider what happens when rates drop by even one full percent: every buyer who has been waiting on the sidelines will jump back into the market at the exact same time. That creates multiple offer wars, pushes purchase prices up by $30,000 to $50,000, and wipes out any interest savings. Right now, you have negotiating power with sellers. You can negotiate price concessions, get closing costs paid, and refinance your rate later when they drop. You marry the home, you date the rate."
Competitor Objection

"Another agent said they can sell it for $30,000 more."

When an agent 'buys the listing' with an unrealistic price promise.

"I don't blame you for being excited by that number. Any agent can tell you a big number to get your signature on a contract today. But an agent doesn't set the price of your home. The market sets the price. If we overprice your home in week one, the most motivated buyers will pass on it, your home will sit on the market, and you will end up chasing the market down with price reductions. My job isn't to tell you what sounds good. My job is to protect your equity and show you the exact numbers verified buyers are paying right now."
Listing Management

The Price Adjustment Conversation

When a home has had 15 showings in 21 days with zero offers.

"Hi [Seller Name], we've had 14 showings over the past three weeks and plenty of online saves, but zero written offers. In real estate, showings with no offers mean one thing: buyers love your home online, but when they walk through in person, they feel the price doesn't match the condition compared to other options in [Neighborhood]. The market is speaking to us. To capture the next wave of fresh buyers this weekend, I recommend adjusting our price to [Target Price]. That will reposition us at the top of buyer searches and trigger new notifications across all major portals."
