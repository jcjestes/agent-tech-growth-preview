Video Inbound Engine

Modern agent video & YouTube setup guide.

Video is the single most powerful client attraction tool in modern real estate. This blueprint removes all gear confusion so you can produce professional 4K videos with equipment you already own or a simple sub-$300 kit.

The Equipment Blueprint (Starter vs Pro)

Audio quality is 10 times more important than camera quality. Viewers will forgive slightly imperfect video, but they will click away within 3 seconds if the audio has echo, wind noise, or static.

The Zero-Budget Smartphone Setup ($25 Total)

  • Camera: Your current iPhone or Android phone (shoot in 4K at 24fps or 30fps)
  • Audio: Boya or PoP voice 3.5mm/Lightning clip-on lavalier mic ($18 on Amazon)
  • Lighting: Face a large open window with natural, indirect sunlight
  • Mount: Sit phone on a coffee mug or inexpensive tripod ($12)
Best for: Launching your first 10 YouTube videos and daily Reels

The Pro Creator Mobile Studio ($250 to $350 Total)

  • Audio: DJI Mic Mini or Rode Wireless ME ($99 - $149) with noise reduction
  • Lighting: 18-inch Neewer Bi-Color Ring Light with phone mount ($79)
  • Tripod: Ulanzi MT-44 extendable tripod with ball head ($32)
  • Lens: Phone wide-angle back lens (never use the low-res selfie camera)
Best for: Crisp studio sit-down videos, neighborhood driving tours, and luxury walkthroughs

The Top 3 Video Editing Software for Agents

  • CapCut (Desktop & Mobile · Free): The gold standard for short-form video. Automatically creates animated captions, trims pauses, and includes royalty-free background music.
  • Descript (Desktop · Free / $12/mo): Edit your video like a Word document. It transcribes your audio into text, and when you delete a word or filler word ("um", "ah") in the script, it automatically cuts it from the video.
  • DaVinci Resolve (Desktop · Free): Hollywood-grade color correction and multi-track audio editing for agents ready for cinematic property tours.
