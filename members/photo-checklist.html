James Jestes Coastal Photography Guide

6 essential tripod & coastal shots for RealWolfPack.com and social media attraction.

Location: Coastal Beach / Boardwalk

Gear: Smartphone (back lens) + Tripod

Part 1: Shots of James (The Mentor & Guide)
[ ] Shot 1: The Anchor Portrait (Most Important)Hero & About Page
Setting: Boardwalk, dunes path, or beach railing with ocean soft in background.
Wardrobe: Navy blazer with open-collar shirt, or crisp dark button-down.
Pose: Looking directly into camera, warm confident smile. Try arms crossed and hands in pockets.
Camera: Chest height. Take both vertical (4:5) and horizontal (16:9).

Setting: Sitting on beach bench or coastal table with iPad or laptop open.
Wardrobe: Modern business casual.

Part 2: Coastal Scenery & B-Roll (No People)

Setting: Looking up at an oceanfront condotel or luxury condo tower.

[ ] Shot 5: Wide Horizon & Breaking WavesBanner Headers

[ ] Shot 6: Palm Trees & Beach ApproachTemplate Covers
Setting: Clean coastal road or wooden dune walkway framed by palm trees.
Composition: Leading lines drawing the eye toward the ocean water.

3 Rules for Great Shots

  • Best Light: Shoot early morning or 1 hour before sunset (golden hour) to avoid harsh overhead eye shadows.
  • Camera: Always use the back lens of your phone (never the lower-res selfie camera). Set to 4K or highest resolution.
  • Safety: Take 4 to 5 variations of each pose with slightly different smiles and head angles so we have plenty of options!
