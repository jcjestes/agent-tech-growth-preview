01 Pre-Recording Setup (5 Minutes)

Before hitting record, ensure your recording tools and talking points are open on your desktop:

1. Screen Recorder: Open Loom, OBS, or QuickTime. Ensure your microphone input is set to your broadcast mic (Shure SM7B or wireless lapel) and webcam bubble is visible in the bottom corner.
2. Open Two Browser Tabs:

Tab 1: realwolfpack.com/members/ai-prompts.html (The 120 AI Mega Prompt Vault)

Tab 2: Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)

3. Video Filming Setup: Mount your smartphone or camera on a tripod at eye level with clean audio and natural window light facing you.
