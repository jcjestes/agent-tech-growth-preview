Database Multiplication

Sphere of influence revival playbook.

Over 70 percent of real estate transactions come from people who already know you or referrals from your sphere. This playbook shows you how to segment your contacts and reactivate them with pure value, without ever feeling like an annoying salesperson.

Step 1: The 4-Tier Database Categorization

Stop blasting the exact same generic real estate newsletter to everyone. Group your phone contacts into these 4 tiers:

Tier A: Raving Fans (10 to 25 people)

Past clients, close friends, and family who actively refer you or talk about real estate with you. Touch frequency: 2 times per month.

Tier B: Warm Advocates (50 to 100 people)

People who like you, know you are in real estate, and would use you if they had a need. Touch frequency: 1 time per month.

Tier C: Acquaintances (100 to 300 people)

Old colleagues, gym friends, high school alumni, and social connections. Touch frequency: 1 quarterly value update.

Tier D: Professional Vendors (20 to 40 people)

Lenders, inspectors, contractors, roofers, CPAs, and divorce attorneys. Touch frequency: Monthly referral check-in.

The "Thinking of You" Text Script

Send this to 5 people in your sphere every Monday morning:

"Hey [Name], I was just thinking about you and wanted to check in! How are things with [job / family / kids]? Hope your year is off to a great start!"

The Local Recommendation Text

Great for starting effortless conversations that naturally turn to real estate:

"Hey [Name], random question: we have a client moving to [Neighborhood/Town] looking for a great local [coffee shop / Italian restaurant / landscaper]. What is your favorite spot around there?"

The Annual Equity Update Offer

The highest converting text to uncover seller intent without asking them to sell:

"Hey [Name], I am updating all my property valuation records for [Neighborhood] this week. Home values have shifted quite a bit over the last year. Would you like me to send you an updated 1-page equity report for your home? No strings attached, just good numbers to have for your taxes and net worth."

The Simple 33-Touch Annual Sphere Calendar

How to stay top of mind 33 times a year without annoying anyone:

  • 12 Monthly Value Emails: Local market stats, seasonal home maintenance tips, or community spotlight newsletters.
  • 8 Personal Text Messages: Birthdays, home purchase anniversaries, thinking of you, and quarterly check-ins.
  • 4 Direct Mail Postcards: Semi-annual neighborhood sales recap, spring cleaning checklist, and holiday greeting.
  • 4 Social Media Engagements: Meaningful comments on their personal life milestones (new baby, promotion, vacation).
  • 4 Phone Calls: Quick 5-minute catch-up calls to say hello and ask how you can support their business.
  • 1 Client Appreciation Event: Summer ice cream social, Thanksgiving pie giveaway, or outdoor movie night.
