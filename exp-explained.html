Brokerage model

Costs and caps

04

Revenue share

Agents may be curious about revenue share and organization growth. This should be explained carefully, with no income promises or implied guarantees.

05

Stock and ownership mindset

06

Community

For the right agent, being around collaborative, modern, tech-friendly, and growth-minded agents can be a major benefit.

Common questions

Questions agents ask before switching.

Is eXp right for every agent?

No. The goal is to understand the model, support, expectations, and whether it fits the agent's goals, market, production, and preferred way of working.

Can this page explain revenue share?

Yes, but carefully. It should educate without implying guaranteed income or typical results. Official eXp materials should be used for current details.

Where do Mike Sherrard videos fit?
