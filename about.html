The Philosophy Behind Real Wolf Pack

I have worked in real estate for decades, and I have worked in the technology trades since the early 1990s. Over the years, I watched the industry get split into two extremes:

On one side, traditional brokerages telling agents to print postcards and cold call phone books like it is 1995. On the other side, online gurus selling $5,000 courses filled with complicated AI buzzwords that overwhelm regular agents.

Neither approach works for real people building real businesses.

Agents need two things to thrive in the modern market: practical transaction mentorship from someone who will pick up the phone when a contract gets sticky, and high-level modern marketing systems that make lead generation predictable.

That is why I aligned with Mike Sherrard and Agent Wolf Pack. Mike built the single best social media, video, and AI training ecosystem in real estate history with over 2,000 agents. I built RealWolfPack.com as my personal portal to bring agents into that organization, while providing hands-on Florida mentorship, custom template packs, and direct 1-on-1 strategy sessions.

Whether you are brand new to the business or an established producer looking for systems and leverage, I would love to talk about what partnering together could look like.
