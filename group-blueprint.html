THE 4-PART OPERATING SYSTEM

How Modern Agents Turn Listings into Inbound Buyers

Most real estate agents post a raw MLS link into Facebook groups and pray someone clicks it. That is the fastest way to get zero views. Here is the actual step-by-step engine high producers use to generate 15 to 30 buyer inquiries from every listing.

01
ALGORITHM MASTERY

Why Facebook Suppresses MLS Links & What to Post Instead

Facebook is in the business of keeping users on Facebook. When you paste an external MLS link or Zillow link directly in your post caption, Facebook's algorithm flags it as an outbound exit link and cuts your reach by up to 90%.

The 3 Posting Rules That Stop the Scroll:
1. The 4-Photo Carousel: Upload 4 high-resolution photos directly to Facebook. Photo 1: Bright exterior curb shot or water view (never a dark front door). Photo 2: Kitchen island/cabinets. Photo 3: Primary suite/bath. Photo 4: Outdoor living / screened lanai / pool.
2. Always State the Exact Price and City: When agents hide the price and write "DM me for price", 90% of qualified buyers keep scrolling because it feels deceptive. Transparency builds authority.
3. The First-Comment Link Strategy: Put your full virtual tour, 3D walkthrough, or custom MLS website link in the first comment of the post. Write in the caption: "Full floorplan and photo gallery linked in the first comment below."
02
AI COPYWRITING WORKFLOW

How to Write Authentic Copy in 30 Seconds with AI

Most agents hate AI copy because it sounds cheesy, robotic, and fake (e.g. "Nestled in an oasis of tranquility..."). But when you give ChatGPT or Claude our Human Voice Guardrails, it writes high-converting, plain-English copy that sounds like a seasoned local Realtor talking over coffee.

PROMPT TEMPLATE 01: THE CANDID OPEN HOUSE STORY
You are a top-producing local Realtor writing a candid, conversational Facebook post for an upcoming open house. 

Here are the property details:
[PASTE PROPERTY ADDRESS, PRICE, BEDS/BATHS, AND 3 TOP FEATURES]

RULES FOR OUTPUT:
1. Zero robotic AI clichés: NEVER use "nestled", "boasts", "oasis", "breathtaking", "rare gem", "sanctuary", or "delve".
2. Write in a warm, direct, first-person tone. Sounds like a knowledgeable local agent texting a friend.
3. Highlight 3 practical lifestyle details (e.g., backyard privacy from neighbors, kitchen layout for entertaining, room to park a boat with no HOA).
4. End with a low-friction trigger word: "Drop a comment with the word DETAILS for room dimensions and seller disclosures."
5. Include standard license compliance: Listed by [Name], [Brokerage].
PROMPT TEMPLATE 02: THE PRICE IMPROVEMENT REALITY CHECK
Write a high-converting Facebook post announcing a price adjustment on a residential home.

Details:
- Old Price: [OLD PRICE]
- New Price: [NEW PRICE]
- Location: [NEIGHBORHOOD, CITY]
- Key Updates: [NEW ROOF, HVAC, POOL, ETC.]

RULES:
- Confident, not desperate. Frame the price drop around the monthly payment savings and zero deferred maintenance.
- Focus on why this is now one of the lowest price-per-square-foot homes in the neighborhood with these features.
- Call to Action: "Comment WALKTHROUGH or send a message to tour it privately before the weekend crowd."
