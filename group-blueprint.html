COMMUNITY ANNOUNCEMENT

Why Most Open House Posts Get Zero Comments (And How to Fix It)

As the founder of Florida Real Estate and Open Houses (now over 35,000 members strong), I review hundreds of posts every week. The single biggest mistake I see agents make is copying an MLS link, dropping it into the group with "Come see this beauty this Sunday!", and wondering why nobody shows up.

Facebook's algorithm hates external links. When you post a raw link, Facebook penalizes the reach because it wants users to stay on Facebook. But when you post a compelling photo carousel, tell a short story about the property, and ask an open-ended question, Facebook pushes your post to hundreds of local buyers for free.

Rule 1: Put your photos directly in the post (3 to 5 high-res photos: curb appeal, kitchen, outdoor space).
Rule 2: State the exact price, neighborhood, and city clearly (buyers scroll past posts with hidden prices).
Rule 3: Put the MLS link or tour link in the first comment, not in the main caption!

Template 2: The "Price Improvement" Inbound Hook

BEST FOR ACTIVE BUYERS

Price adjustments get huge organic reach when framed around monthly payment affordability.

🚨 PRICE ADJUSTMENT in [City / Neighborhood]!

Sellers just adjusted the price by $[Amount Dropped] on this [Bed/Bath count] home in [Neighborhood].

📍 Location: [City, State]
💰 New Price: $[New Price] (Was $[Old Price])
📐 Sq Footage: [Total SqFt]

Why this stands out in today's market:
1. [Key Feature 1, e.g. Just 8 minutes to the beach / oceanfront]
2. [Key Feature 2, e.g. Brand new 2026 roof and AC unit]
3. [Key Feature 3, e.g. Low HOA fee including lawn maintenance]

Private showings available all week. Comment "TOUR" or send a direct message for complete property records and seller disclosures!

Template 3: The "Investor & Short-Term Rental" Numbers Hook
