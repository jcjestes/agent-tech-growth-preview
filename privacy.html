Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

RealWolfPack.com is operated by James Jestes, Broker Associate with eXp Realty. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with RealWolfPack.com, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and geographic market.
  • Professional Information: Current real estate brokerage, production level, years of experience, and business goals.
  • Inquiry Details: Questions, deal review notes, or messages submitted through our intake and mentorship forms.
  • Authentication Data: Email and account credentials created when registering for the member portal.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and scheduling confidential partnership conversations with James Jestes.
  • Providing access to the Real Wolf Pack member hub, training tracks, and template resources.
  • Communicating updates regarding weekly mastermind calls, new training modules, and organization news.
  • Evaluating organization fit and supporting onboarding for agents who join our eXp organization.

3. Information Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party marketers or external lead brokers under any circumstances. Information is kept confidential between you, James Jestes, and authorized leadership within our organization.

4. Data Storage and Security

Our website and member portal utilize Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and role-based security rules to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Links and Resources

Our training library and resources may contain links to external platforms, including Google Drive, Canva, YouTube, and official eXp Realty tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party websites.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the removal of your contact information, please contact James Jestes directly at james@jamesjestes.com.
