The Golden Rules for 100% Human AI Real Estate Copy

Most agents hate AI copy because it sounds cheesy, robotic, and fake. Every prompt in this vault has been engineered with our Human Voice Protocol so outputs sound like a real, experienced local agent talking to a client over coffee.

BANNED ROBOT CLICHES (Never Allowed in Our Outputs): "Nestled", "boasts", "oasis", "breathtaking", "rare gem", "tapestry", "sanctuary", "delve", "furthermore", "meticulously maintained", "step inside and be amazed".
WHAT WE USE INSTEAD: Concrete sensory details (morning light, parking a boat, walking 3 blocks to dinner), real numbers, conversational pacing, and genuine plain English.
