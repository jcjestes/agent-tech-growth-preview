JAMES JESTES · STR & CONDOTEL PERSPECTIVE

"I talk about Airbnbs and condotels extensively on Salt Air Sessions on YouTube. Here is why agents need this in their business."

Real estate agents talk to second-home buyers and vacation rental investors every single week. But most agents only know how to open a lockbox. They do not know how to analyze rental occupancy, evaluate condotel HOA reserve budgets, navigate short-term rental zoning, or advise clients on operational management.

I have extensive hands-on experience in the sale, purchase, and property management of short-term rentals and condotels across coastal Florida. When you understand both sides of the transaction, you don't just close one-off deals. You become a trusted advisor to investors who buy multiple properties and rely on your guidance.

When you partner with our organization at eXp Realty, I personally coach you on working with short-term rental investors, analyzing condotel deals, and building management workflows that set your business apart.

1. Condotels & Coastal STRs

Learn how to navigate HOA restrictions, non-warrantable condo financing, and local municipal regulations to protect your clients.

2. Investor Deal Analysis

Present accurate cap rates, seasonal occupancy estimates, and revenue projections that turn casual buyers into confident investors.

3. Turnkey Management Workflows

Understand the tech stack behind automated messaging, dynamic pricing algorithms, and turnover operations for vacation rentals.
